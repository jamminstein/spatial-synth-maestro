Start with listening. The rest can come a little at a time. This guide describes the current app experience as it is prepared for Apple Vision Pro.

Your first room

  1. Open the app and choose Begin listening. The welcome window closes after the mixed-reality room opens.
  2. Let the orchestra play. You can stay seated and listen without making a gesture.
  3. Use Controls on the small strip in the room to open the readable controls window. Pause stops the sound; Play resumes it.

The first coach tour covers six essentials. Pinch the coach to skip a step, or choose End tour under Help and comfort. Guided tour offers a longer visit when you are ready.

Hand tracking and the microphone are optional. You can choose a drawing and shape it through the controls window.

Playing a voice

Look at a drawing and pinch once to select it. Open that hand, then move gently: sideways changes tone, up and down changes pulse, and reaching changes space. A second hand can take another drawing.

Keep pinching while you drag to move a drawing. Pinch empty air with the selecting hand to let go, or use Let go in Controls.

In the window, Choose a drawing gives you tone, pulse, and space sliders. Sound-generating drawings also offer Mute and Solo. The room keeps developing around what you play.

Shape the room offers tempo, recentering, and six moods: In the Body, Sideways Smile, Unhurried, Soft Overflow, Elsewhere, and Far Within. A new piece starts a different composition; the app asks before replacing the current room.

Let the room answer you

Enable the microphone under Voice and recording, or select Aftervoice to request permission. With tracked hands, select Aftervoice, open your hand to capture a phrase, then close your hand to finish it. Without custom hand tracking, phrase detection creates the loop.

The loop is shaped to the piece's scale. Mic off stops microphone input. Microphone audio stays on the headset unless you record a performance containing the singing and then choose to share that recording.

Saved scenes do not include singing loops. See the microphone privacy details.

Keep a scene. Revisit a piece.

Under Keep a scene, save one of three slots and give it a name. A scene keeps the composition, mix, voice settings, and drawing layout. Replacing a saved scene requires confirmation. A scene is not an audio recording.

Copy piece seed copies the title and seed of the generated composition. Paste it into Replay seed to rebuild that starting composition. The seed does not capture your later performance or edits.

Older scene formats can recover their mix and layout into the current piece. Their original composition cannot be recovered if the old save did not contain a seed; the original save is retained.

Keep a little of the moment

Choose Record a performance, then Stop recording when you are finished. A take lasts up to one minute. Pausing or leaving the room also finishes an active take.

Finished WAVs appear in Recordings, with dates and durations, and stay in the library after relaunching. Preview plays only when you choose it and pauses the orchestra. Stop preview before returning to live play.

Use More → Rename to give a take a name. Share audio through the system share sheet; a matching app video can also be shared when the system has allowed one to be recorded. Video is not guaranteed.

Move to Trash is recoverable. Use Undo immediately or open Trash and choose Restore later. A matching video follows the audio. Trash stays on the device and is not automatically emptied.

The WAV is a stereo keepsake. It may include your singing, and it does not reproduce the headset's full spatial filtering and room reverb.

If something feels off

The room did not open

The welcome window remains available after a cancellation or error. Close another immersive app if necessary and choose Begin listening or Return to the room again.

I cannot hear anything

Check that playback is not paused, increase the app volume gently, and check your headset or connected output. Select a drawing and check Mute and Solo. After an interruption or output disconnection, choose Play deliberately. If the audio system still cannot resume, leave the room and reopen the app.

A gesture is difficult or is not recognized

Open Controls and use Choose a drawing and the sliders instead. Hand tracking is optional. Bring room to me recenters the drawings. High contrast ink and Reduce motion are available under Help and comfort.

The microphone is not available

Enable microphone access in the app's system settings if you want to use Aftervoice, then return to the app. Resume the orchestra before enabling singing. You can keep listening and playing other voices without microphone permission.

I moved a recording to Trash

Open Recordings → Trash and choose Restore beside the take. The app retains both the audio and any matching video. A restored file receives a distinct filename if another file occupies its original location.

I want to report an audio or performance problem

Open About and diagnostics, create the local report, and review it before sharing. It contains technical status, not audio, hand poses, saved scenes, or piece seeds. No report is sent automatically. Include what you were doing and whether a particular output device was connected.

Website sound studies

The homepage’s instrument drawings and sounds come from the app’s native drawing and synthesis engines, rendered offline into six seven-second studies and a 42-second film. These are studio presentations, not headset footage or demonstrations of the final spatial-audio experience. They contain no microphone input or user recordings. The “elsewhere” portal is AI-generated concept artwork, not an app screenshot or headset footage.

Choosing a drawing or changing the instrument selector stays silent. Use the native audio or video player’s Play control to listen; playing one pauses the other. The film includes English sound-description captions. A direct file link is available if the embedded player cannot load.

Each visit starts with a new decorative print. “Misprint again” changes only the illustration and handwriting; it preserves your selected instrument and playback state. Pointer movement gently shifts the layered illustrations. Touch and Reduced Motion keep them still. The visual distortions do not change the readable text supplied to assistive technology.
