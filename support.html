Your first room

  1. Choose Begin listening. Six voices begin together: rhythm, bass, harmony, sequence, melody, and texture.
  2. Listen first. The band develops by itself; you can stay seated.
  3. Open Controls from the small room strip. Pause stops the sound; Play resumes it.

The short guide introduces listening, approaching a voice, shaping it, and letting go. Replay Short guide or choose Hide guide under Help and comfort.

Hand tracking and the microphone are optional. Controls offers the same sound parameters without custom hand tracking.

Joining a voice

Bring one hand near a drawing and linger. The drawing gathers your hand, then joins on the musical clock. A quick pass does not take over a voice. Opening and curling your fingers, turning your palm, and moving gently shape the sound. Changes ease in; moving away lets go. A second hand can join another voice.

In Controls, Choose a voice opens Tone, Pulse, and Space sliders, plus Mute, Solo, and Let go. Shape the room offers tempo, recentering, and moods. A new piece starts a different composition after confirmation.

An Undo button appears after supported deliberate control changes, including a new piece or recalling a scene. It restores one previous change, not a complete performance history. Room restoration does not recover singing loops or recordings.

Let the room answer you

Choose Enable microphone under Voice and recording. Aftervoice takes the texture voice's place; input begins only with permission. Select it, open your hand to capture a phrase, then close your hand to finish it. Without custom hand tracking, phrase detection creates the loop.

The loop is shaped to the piece's scale. Mic off stops input. Microphone audio stays on the headset unless you record a performance containing the singing and then choose to share it.

Scenes do not include singing loops. See the microphone privacy details.

Keep a scene. Revisit a piece.

Under Keep a scene, save one of three slots and give it a name. A scene keeps the composition, mix, voice settings, and drawing layout. Replacing a saved scene requires confirmation. A scene is not an audio recording.

Copy piece seed copies the title and seed of the generated composition. Paste it into Replay seed to rebuild that starting composition. The seed does not capture your later performance or edits.

Older scene formats can recover their mix and layout into the current piece. Their original composition cannot be recovered if the old save did not contain a seed; the original save is retained.

Keep a little of the moment

Choose Record a performance, then Stop recording when you are finished. A take lasts up to one minute. Pausing or leaving the room also finishes an active take.

Finished WAVs appear in Recordings, with dates and durations, and stay in the library after relaunching. Preview plays only when you choose it and pauses the band. Stop preview before returning to live play.

Use More → Rename to give a take a name. Share audio through the system share sheet; a matching app video can also be shared when the system has allowed one to be recorded. Video is not guaranteed.

Move to Trash is recoverable. Use Undo immediately or open Trash and choose Restore later. A matching video follows the audio. Trash stays on the device and is not automatically emptied.

The WAV is a stereo keepsake. It may include your singing, and it does not reproduce the headset's full spatial filtering and room reverb.

If something feels off

The room did not open

The welcome window remains available after a cancellation or error. Close another immersive app if necessary and choose Begin listening or Return to the room again.

I cannot hear anything

Check that playback is not paused, increase the app volume gently, and check your headset or connected output. Select a drawing and check Mute and Solo. After an interruption or output disconnection, choose Play deliberately. If the audio system still cannot resume, leave the room and reopen the app.

A gesture is difficult or is not recognized

Open Controls and use Choose a voice and the sliders instead. Hand tracking is optional. Bring room to me recenters the drawings. High contrast ink and Reduce motion are available under Help and comfort.

The microphone is not available

Enable microphone access in the app's system settings if you want to use Aftervoice, then return to the app. Resume the band before enabling singing. You can keep listening and playing other voices without microphone permission.

I moved a recording to Trash

Open Recordings → Trash and choose Restore beside the take. The app retains both the audio and any matching video. A restored file receives a distinct filename if another file occupies its original location.

I want to report an audio or performance problem

Open About and diagnostics, create the local report, and review it before sharing. It contains technical status, not audio, hand poses, saved scenes, or piece seeds. No report is sent automatically. Include what you were doing and whether a particular output device was connected.

Website sound studies

The homepage pairs native drawings and synthesis in a 64-second studio film: a 16-second ensemble opening, followed by six eight-second voice portraits. The opening uses the app's actual audio graph; portraits let you hear each engine alone. These are offline renders, not headset footage or the final spatial-audio experience. No microphone input or user recordings are included. The three imagined environments are AI-generated concept artwork.

Choose a drawing to move quietly to its film chapter, then press Play to listen. Grabbing a decorative hand and moving it across drawings can play short, low-volume native sound cues; the speaker control mutes these. Keyboard users can grab with Space, move with the arrows, release with Escape, and return the hand with Home. Film and hand sounds take turns. Sound-description captions and a direct film file are available.

Each visit makes a new print. “Misprint again” changes its illustration and handwriting. The distortions preserve readable labels for assistive technology. Reduced Motion limits decorative movement.
