Modifications for Spatial Synth Maestro — 2026-11-02
- Consolidated scene/material draws and simplified the complete headset and band.
  151,469 source triangles became 35,769 triangles; no component was removed.
- Quantized supported vertex attributes using glTF Transform 4.5.1.
- Embedded every texture as WebP. Most maps are 256px or smaller; four key
  visor/band maps retain 512px detail. A uniform map was reduced to one pixel.
- Centered the model, normalized its longest extent to 1, oriented its visor
  toward +Z and its top toward +Y. All textures and geometry are inside the GLB.
- Rendered a transparent 512px fallback from the same optimized model in Blender.
