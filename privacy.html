Privacy policy.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Spatial Synth Maestro is a music app for Apple Vision Pro. It does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting service.

Information the developer receives

The app does not automatically send microphone audio, tracking data, saved scenes, recordings, diagnostics, or usage information to the developer. It does not use an advertising identifier, track you across apps, or provide a cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

The app stores information on your device to make the instrument work. Local storage is separate from data collection by the developer. If you choose to email support or share a recording or diagnostics report, the information you send reaches the recipient you choose.

Hands, head, and surroundings

If you allow hand tracking, hand poses are processed on the headset to select and play drawings. Head tracking helps position the orchestra and its controls. The app does not save raw hand- or head-tracking streams or transmit them to the developer. Saved scenes can contain the positions you arrange for drawings, and an app video may include visualizations of your performance.

Hand tracking is optional. The orchestra can play on its own, and the native controls window provides selection and sound controls. If a tracking permission is declined, the available interactions adapt. The app does not scan room meshes or planes.

Microphone and singing

The microphone is optional and is requested when you choose to enable it or select Aftervoice. If allowed, microphone audio is processed on the headset to detect singing and breath and to create a musical loop. The loop is held in app memory. It is not included in saved scenes and is not automatically sent anywhere.

If you record a performance, its sound can include the Aftervoice loop and therefore your voice. That performance is saved to a local audio file. Use Mic off in Controls to stop microphone input. Microphone access can also be changed in system settings.

Saved scenes and preferences

The app stores tutorial completion, preferences, scene names, and up to three scene snapshots in its local preferences. Scenes include composition identifiers, musical settings, instrument states, and drawing layouts. They do not contain microphone samples or singing loops.

Recordings and the local library

When you choose to record, the app saves a stereo WAV file in its local Documents directory. Recordings last up to one minute and can be stopped earlier. If supported and allowed by the system, recording may also create a local app video using Apple's ReplayKit.

The recording library stores recording names, dates, durations, and file references locally. You can rename recordings, explicitly preview their audio, share them, or move them to Trash. Preview does not start automatically. A matching app video, when present, follows the audio when it is renamed, moved to Trash, or restored.

Trash is recoverable local storage, not permanent deletion. Recordings in Trash remain on the device and can be restored. The app does not automatically empty Trash. Local app data is removed when the app and its data are deleted.

Sharing opens the system share sheet. A recording leaves the app when you choose to send or export it to another app, person, or service. The destination's privacy practices then apply. Copies you export remain wherever you save or send them. Device backup and restore behavior is controlled by your Apple device settings and services.

Optional diagnostics

You can create a local diagnostics report from About and diagnostics. It contains technical state such as app and operating-system versions, audio status, active voice count, render timing warnings, thermal state, and whether optional inputs and comfort settings are enabled.

The report excludes audio, hand or head poses, saved scenes, and piece seeds. It is not created as an automatic upload. You choose whether to share the report and with whom. If you send it to support, it is used to investigate the issue you report.

Clipboard and piece seeds

Copy piece seed places the current piece title and seed on the system pasteboard. You can paste that text elsewhere to share a composition. Replay seed reads the text you enter into its field. Seeds describe a generated composition; they do not include audio recordings, singing loops, or tracking streams.

This support website

This website has no added advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in, or contact form. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages information and GitHub's Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's on-device processing.

Children's privacy

The app does not automatically collect personal information from children or other users for the developer. Optional local recording and user-chosen sharing behave as described above.

Changes and contact

Policy updates will use a new date and be published at jamminstein.github.io/spatial-synth-maestro/privacy.html.

For privacy questions or support, email tiagopereira@me.com. If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your request.
